About our business

Care for every thread

Saree Skill and Re-Polish Center offers saree repair, restoration, maintenance and beautification. Whether it is a family heirloom, a wedding saree or a favourite everyday drape, we look after it with patience and skill.

We always begin by looking at your saree and explaining, in simple words, what we can do for it.

Featured Services

A few of the ways we can help. We offer ten services in all.

Saree Re-Polishing

Give a tired, dull saree a fresher, cared-for look.

Over time, even a well-kept saree can look dull, stiff or tired. Re-polishing is our service to refresh its overall appearance and finish so it looks cared-for again.

Full details

Saree Darning

Neat, careful mending of small holes, snags and tears.

A small hole or snag does not have to mean the end of a beloved saree. Darning is careful hand mending that closes small damage neatly.

Full details

Saree Embroidery

Hand and machine embroidery to add beauty or cover marks.

Embroidery adds beauty and personality to a saree. It can also be used to decorate plain sarees or neatly cover small marks.

Full details

Saree Restoration

Complete care for old, damaged or heirloom sarees.

Some sarees carry memories. Restoration is our complete care service for old, worn or damaged sarees that deserve to be saved.

Full details

Saree Zari Work Restoration

Care for tarnished, loose or worn zari work.

Zari is the golden thread work that makes many sarees special. When it becomes loose, worn or dull, we help restore its appearance with careful work.

Full details

Saree Colour Restoration

Bring faded or patchy colour closer to its original look.

Faded patches, uneven colour or dull areas can make a beautiful saree look old. We work to bring the colour closer to how it used to look.
