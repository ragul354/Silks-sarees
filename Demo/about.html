About Us

Restore the Beauty. Preserve the Tradition.

Our story

Who we are

Saree Skill and Re-Polish Center is dedicated to repairing, restoring and caring for sarees. Many sarees carry memories of weddings, festivals and loved ones, and we believe they deserve careful handling.

Our work covers re-polishing, dyeing, darning, fall and pico, embroidery, border repair, zari work, colour restoration and everyday cleaning and maintenance.

Our expertise

What We Do Best

R

Repair

Darning, border repair and fall and pico to fix wear and tear.

B

Beautification

Embroidery, dyeing and re-polishing to refresh the look of a saree.

P

Preservation

Restoration, zari and colour care, plus cleaning and storage advice.

Mission & vision

What Guides Us

Our Mission

To give every saree careful, honest and skilled attention, so that customers can keep wearing and enjoying the sarees they love.

Our Vision

To be a trusted name for saree care, where people know their saree will be handled with respect and explained to them clearly.

Our commitment

Committed to Saree Restoration

We look at each saree on its own. Before any work starts, we explain what we see, what we can do and what to expect. We never promise results that we cannot stand behind.

Results depend on the age, fabric and condition of each saree, and we will always be open with you about this.
