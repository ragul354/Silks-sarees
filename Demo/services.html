Saree Re-Polishing

Over time, even a well-kept saree can look dull, stiff or tired. Re-polishing is our service to refresh its overall appearance and finish so it looks cared-for again.

What is saree re-polishing?

Re-polishing is a refresh service for a saree that has lost its freshness. We carefully clean and finish the saree so that it looks neater and feels more pleasant to wear. It is not a repair of tears or holes; it is about the overall look and finish.

Why is it needed?

Sarees kept in cupboards for years, or worn many times, can look dull, creased or flat. Re-polishing is useful before a wedding, festival or family function, or when you want to bring a cherished saree back into use.

How we do it, step by step

  1. You show us the saree and we inspect the fabric, colour, zari and borders.
  2. We explain what we can do and what to expect, and agree the work with you.
  3. The saree is gently cleaned and prepared using methods suited to its fabric.
  4. We finish and press the saree with care, paying attention to borders and zari.
  5. We check the finished saree together with you before handing it back.

Benefits

  • A fresher, neater look
  • Helps old sarees get back into use
  • Useful before weddings and festivals
  • Honest advice before any work starts

Suitable saree materials

  • Silk
  • Cotton
  • Cotton-silk
  • Georgette
  • Chiffon
  • Crepe

Service 03

Saree Darning

A small hole or snag does not have to mean the end of a beloved saree. Darning is careful hand mending that closes small damage neatly.

What is saree darning?

Darning is a mending skill where the damaged area is repaired by carefully working thread into it so the hole or tear is closed. It is done by hand and is best for small to medium damage.

Why is it needed?

Holes can come from moth or insect damage, snagging, wear at the folds or accidents. Left alone, small damage can grow larger, so early mending helps.

How we do it, step by step

  1. We look closely at the damaged area and the fabric around it.
  2. We choose thread that matches the saree as closely as possible.
  3. The weak area is prepared so the repair can sit neatly.
  4. The hole or tear is carefully darned by hand.
  5. We press the area and check that the repair blends in as well as it can.

Benefits

  • Stops small damage from spreading
  • Keeps the saree wearable
  • Hand-finished for a neat look

Suitable saree materials

  • Silk
  • Cotton
  • Georgette
  • Chiffon

Service 04

Saree Fall and Pico

A good fall and a neat pico edge help a saree hang well and last longer. We stitch both with care.

What is saree fall and pico?

A saree fall is a strip of fabric stitched along the bottom inside edge to give the saree weight and protect it from wear. Pico is a fine, neat finishing stitch along the edge of the saree so it does not fray.

Why is it needed?

Without a fall, the lower edge of a saree wears out quickly from rubbing against the floor and footwear. Pico edging stops loose threads and frayed edges.

How we do it, step by step

  1. We check the edge of your saree and measure the length needed.
  2. We choose a fall fabric in a colour that suits your saree.
  3. The fall is stitched along the bottom edge.
  4. The saree's edge is finished with pico stitching.
  5. We press the saree and check that the edge lies flat and even.

Benefits

  • Protects the lower edge from wear
  • Helps the saree hang better
  • Clean, finished look
  • Quick and affordable upkeep

Suitable saree materials

  • Silk
  • Cotton
  • Georgette
  • Chiffon
  • Synthetic sarees

Yes. We can remove the old fall and stitch a new one.

Yes, we can do pico on sarees you already own, as long as the edge is in a suitable condition.

Saree Border Repair

The border is the most noticeable and most worn part of many sarees. We repair frayed, torn or loose borders so your saree looks tidy again.

What is saree border repair?

Border repair means fixing damage to the decorative edge of a saree, such as frayed threads, torn sections, or a border that has come loose from the body of the saree.

Why is it needed?

Borders take the most strain: they rub against the floor, catch on things and carry the weight of heavy designs. Repairing the border early helps protect the rest of the saree.

How we do it, step by step

  1. We examine the border and the area where it joins the saree.
  2. We check whether the damage is small, or whether a section needs rework.
  3. Loose or frayed parts are secured and prepared.
  4. The border is re-stitched or repaired by hand or machine, as suits the saree.
  5. We press and check the border so it lies flat and even.

Benefits

  • Prevents damage from spreading
  • Keeps borders neat and secure
  • Helps preserve the whole saree

Saree Zari Work Restoration

Zari is the golden thread work that makes many sarees special. When it becomes loose, worn or dull, we help restore its appearance with careful work.

What is saree zari work restoration?

Zari work uses shiny metallic-looking thread in borders, pallus and motifs. Zari restoration means caring for this part of the saree: securing loose zari, repairing worn sections and working to improve its look where possible.

Why is it needed?

Zari can lose its shine, snag, loosen or wear off over the years, especially at folds and borders. Attending to it early helps keep the design intact.

How we do it, step by step

  1. We inspect the zari areas closely to see what condition they are in.
  2. We explain which parts can be secured, repaired or improved.
  3. Loose threads are carefully secured so they do not catch or pull further.
  4. Worn sections are repaired using suitable thread where this is possible.
  5. We check the finished zari work with you and give care advice.

Benefits

  • Helps keep zari work secure
  • Improves the look of worn sections where possible
  • Protects detailed designs
  • Care advice to keep zari looking its best

Saree Colour Restoration

Faded patches, uneven colour or dull areas can make a beautiful saree look old. We work to bring the colour closer to how it used to look.

What is saree colour restoration?

Colour restoration is a careful service that aims to improve faded or uneven colour on a saree. The goal is to make the saree look more even and closer to its original shade.

Why is it needed?

Sunlight, age, washing and storage can all cause colour to fade or become patchy, especially along folds. Colour restoration can make the saree look more uniform again.

How we do it, step by step

  2. We match the closest shade we can to the saree's original colour.
  3. We tell you what level of improvement is realistic for your saree.

  5. We finish the saree and compare it with you to see how it looks.

Benefits
